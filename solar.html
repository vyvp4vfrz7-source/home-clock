太陽系大遊行The Solar System racing through the galaxy

模擬年份 Year2026
太陽帶住八大行星，以每秒 230 公里繞銀河系中心飛行，行一圈要 2.3 億年。行星今日嘅位置係真嘅，1 年 = 6 秒。
The Sun carries the planets round the Milky Way at 230 km/s; one lap takes 230 million years. Planets start where they really are today. 1 year = 6 seconds (distances squeezed to fit).
