# Home Clock

A full-screen clock, Northwood (London) weather, indoor temperature from Apple Home, family photos, Hong Kong + UK headlines and a Cantonese spoken summary for a wall-mounted iPad.

- Weather: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), free, no key. If the device cannot reach it, the page uses `weather.json`, saved every 15 minutes by `.github/workflows/weather.yml`.
- Headlines: `.github/workflows/news.yml` runs `tools/build_news.py` every 30 minutes and writes `news.json` (RTHK 中文 + English local news, BBC 中文, BBC UK) with a short summary, a picture and the place each story is about.
- Photos: list them in `photos.json` as `[{"src":"photos/beach.jpg","text":"Brighton day out","when":"Oct 2026"}]` and put the files in `photos/`.
- Videos: videos in the shared album are copied too (`tools/build_photos.py` saves Apple's small web copy, about 0.5 MB a second, and skips any over 40 MB; the poster is Apple's preview JPEG) and listed as `{"src":"photos/<id>.jpg","video":"photos/<id>.mp4",…}`. The slideshow plays them muted (iPad Safari only autoplays silent video) and moves on when the video ends. Live Photos stay as stills.
- Settings (city, Home buttons, night-clock hours) are in the `CONFIG` block at the top of `index.html`.
- Indoor temperature: an Apple Shortcut ("Send home temp") reads the temperature from Apple Home and calls the GitHub API (`repository_dispatch`, event `home-temp`). `.github/workflows/home-temp.yml` saves it to `home.json`, which the page reads every 5 minutes. The reading greys out after 3 hours and hides after 12.
- Pages: swipe left/right (or tap the dots at the bottom) to move between the dashboard, this week's calendar, a big pink analog clock and the Premier League table. The iPad remembers the last page; `?page=2` in the address opens a given page. Add a page by adding another `<div class="page">` inside `#track` in `index.html`.
- Premier League table: `.github/workflows/table.yml` runs `tools/build_table.py` every hour and writes `table.json` (ESPN's public standings feed, no key). If the feed fails, the last saved table stays.
- Warm-up button (暖氣): the yellow button on the clock runs the Apple Shortcut **Warm Up Home** on the iPad (`shortcuts://run-shortcut?name=Warm%20Up%20Home`). A web page can't change Apple Home itself, so the Shortcut does it: make a Home scene "Warm Up" that sets every heater to the temperature you want, then a Shortcut with one "Control Home → Warm Up" action. The Shortcuts app must be installed on the iPad (free, App Store); with iCloud sync on, a Shortcut made on the iPhone appears on the iPad. Change the label or Shortcut name in `CONFIG.heat`.
- Calendar (日程): `.github/workflows/calendar.yml` runs `tools/build_calendar.py` every 30 minutes and writes `calendar.json` with the next two weeks of events (repeating events included). Put the calendar's private link in the repository secret `CALENDAR_ICS` (iCloud: Calendar app → share the calendar → Public Calendar → copy the `webcal://` link; Google: Settings → the calendar → "Secret address in iCal format"). Several calendars: one link per line, optionally `link | #colour`. To keep events private, also set the secret `CALENDAR_KEY` to a passcode: `calendar.json` is then scrambled (AES-GCM), and the iPad asks for the passcode once (tap the calendar page) and remembers it. Without `CALENDAR_KEY` the event titles are readable by anyone. Until `CALENDAR_ICS` is set the page shows sample events. The calendar has its own page (page 2): the next two weeks, this week on top and next week below. Sports in an event title (football, swimming, tennis, badminton, running, basketball… in English or Chinese) get a small moving strip or hopping icon; festivals and birthdays (Christmas, Chinese New Year, Mid-Autumn, New Year, Halloween, Easter…) get a coloured banner and a little scene on that day. Festival dates in `FEST` in `index.html` show up even when they aren't in the calendar (lunar dates listed to 2028). Animations pause on the other pages. Everyday events (dentist, school, flights, meals, calls, shopping, bins, haircuts…) get their own moving icon too, and anything else a small sparkle. Each day also shows its weather forecast (14 days ahead) (icon, high/low, chance of rain).
- Main page: clock and weather on top, the next 12 hours, the next 5 days (未來五日) and the family photos. Headlines live on the news page.
- News page (page 4): a world map (`world.svg`, Natural Earth via world-atlas) with a pin where each story happened, and one story at a time below it (picture, headline, summary), changing every 10 seconds; tap the story to skip. Its ▶ 播放 button reads the Cantonese summary (weather, today's events, headlines). Places are guessed from words in the headline (`PLACES` in `tools/build_news.py`); RTHK stories with no other place named are pinned on Hong Kong, BBC UK ones on the UK. Feeds: RTHK (香港, 大中華, 國際 in Chinese; Hong Kong in English), BBC 中文, BBC UK, Asia, US & Canada, Europe, Australia, Latin America and Africa (`FEEDS`). A list of the next 16 headlines runs down the middle. The right column shows a world news brief (English and 香港中文, one section every 12 s; tap EN / 中 to switch) written at about 6am and 6pm by a Claude scheduled task ("Home clock news brief"), which saves `brief.json` on the `brief` branch; the page reads it from raw.githubusercontent.com. `brief.json` on main is only a sample shown until the first real one exists.
- Pages turn by themselves: dashboard, week, clock and news each show for a minute (`CONFIG.rotate`). Touching the screen restarts the wait; after a swipe to the league table it stays there 5 minutes. The page also asks search engines not to list it (`robots.txt` + a noindex tag).
- Updates: the page checks every 5 minutes whether `index.html` on GitHub has changed and reloads itself onto the new version, so the Home Screen app picks up merged changes without a manual refresh.
- Birthdays (生日倒數): the last page counts down to family birthdays. Put the list in the repository secret `BIRTHDAYS`, one person per line: `name | date | place | emoji`, e.g. `嫲嫲 | 農曆 1950-08-15 | 香港 | 👵` or `仔仔 | 2016-05-02` (the year is optional and gives the age; `農曆`/`lunar` means a Chinese lunar birthday, worked out each year). `.github/workflows/birthdays.yml` runs `tools/build_birthdays.py` twice a day and writes `birthdays.json`, scrambled with the `CALENDAR_KEY` passcode, so the names and dates aren't readable on GitHub and the iPad that unlocked the calendar opens it too. Until `BIRTHDAYS` is set the page shows sample birthdays. The page joins the turning pages when the next birthday is 14 days away or less (`CONFIG.birthdaySoon`); on the day it shows 生日快樂 with confetti. For someone in Hong Kong it reminds you how far ahead HK time is.
- Family message board (留言板): family post a note from their phone and it pops up big on the iPad over whatever page is showing (held until 7am at night, when the night clock shows "📌 n 個新留言"). Tap 收到 to close it; the newest message then stays as a sticky note on the clock for 2 days. Tap the sticky note to see every message, post one from the iPad, or remove one (✕). Phones post with `post.html` (open https://vyvp4vfrz7-source.github.io/home-clock/post.html, Share → Add to Home Screen, pick a name, paste the GitHub key once) or with an Apple Shortcut "留言板": *Ask for Input* (or *Dictate Text*) → *Get Contents of URL* `https://api.github.com/repos/vyvp4vfrz7-source/home-clock/dispatches`, Method POST, Headers `Authorization: Bearer <key>` and `Accept: application/vnd.github+json`, Request Body JSON `event_type` = `board-post`, `client_payload` (Dictionary) `who` = `媽媽`, `text` = Provided Input. `.github/workflows/board.yml` runs `tools/save_board.py`, which keeps the newest 30 messages from the last 14 days in `board.json`. The message is read from the event file, so it never appears in the public run log, and when the `CALENDAR_KEY` secret is set `board.json` is scrambled with the same passcode as the calendar (the iPad already knows it). The GitHub key can change the site, so only put it on family phones. To wipe the board: Actions → Family message board → Run workflow, type 1.
